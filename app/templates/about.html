Purvanchal Old Age Pension Scheme

Administered by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal

The Old Age Pension Scheme is a state-funded social assistance initiative designed to provide financial independence and dignity to elderly citizens of Purvanchal. Monthly pensions are disbursed directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Key Scheme Features & Statutory SLA

Parameter Statutory Provision
Monthly Pension Amount ₹1,000 per month (Direct Benefit Transfer via PFMS/DBT)
Minimum Qualifying Age 60 years and above as on the date of application submission.
Statutory Processing SLA 15 calendar days under the Purvanchal Right to Public Services Act (RTPS).
Deemed Approval Mechanism Any application pending beyond 15 days is automatically DEEMED_APPROVED by statute.
Application Quota Rule Strictly one active (PENDING / APPROVED / DEEMED_APPROVED) application per mobile number.

Accepted Age Proof Documents

  • Aadhaar Card: Issued by UIDAI showing complete Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
  • Electoral Photo Identity Card (EPIC / Voter ID): Issued by the Election Commission of India.
  • School Leaving Certificate: From a recognized educational board.
  • Gaon Bura / Gram Panchayat Certificate: Signed and stamped by the village head.
  • Birth Certificate: Registered under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act.
