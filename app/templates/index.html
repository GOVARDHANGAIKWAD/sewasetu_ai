How to Apply for Pension in 4 Simple Steps

Designed for senior citizens with simplified verification

Step 1
Mobile & OTP Verification

Enter your 10-digit mobile number and solve a simple security check. Receive instant 6-digit OTP via SMS.

Step 2
Personal & Bank Details

Provide full name, date of birth, village, block, and your single or joint bank account number with IFSC code.

Step 3
Upload Age Proof

Upload a clear photo or PDF of your Aadhaar card, Voter ID, or School Leaving certificate (up to 5 MB).

Step 4
Preview & Acknowledgment

Review the application preview, submit the declaration, and download your official computer-generated acknowledgment receipt.
